Smart Elephant – Engineering Roadmap

Purpose

Smart Elephant is both:

1. A production-quality educational platform.
2. An AI Full Stack Engineering roadmap.

The roadmap intentionally prioritizes backend engineering,
infrastructure, AI integration, and system design while using AI tools
to accelerate repetitive frontend development.

Core Principle

Never introduce infrastructure "just to learn it".
Every technology must solve a real product problem.

Approximately:

20% Frontend Engineering

Interview Goal

By completing this roadmap, the developer will gain practical experience
with modern Full Stack technologies through one coherent production
project instead of isolated tutorials.
