Cozy interior of a children's treehouse playroom, background illustration for a preschool
educational game. High-quality 3D animated family-film style, soft rounded shapes, warm
cinematic lighting, vibrant but natural colors, storybook atmosphere.

Warm honey-colored wooden plank walls and floor, a large round arched window in the upper
center with a view of a bright blue sky, soft clouds, sunshine and green tree leaves outside.
A low wooden shelf on the left with a few small plush toys, a round soft yellow rug in the
center of the floor, a coral-red toy chest on the right, a few hanging paper lanterns or
bunting, small plants. Golden sunlight streaming through the window.

IMPORTANT composition rules:
- NO characters, NO people, NO animals, NO text, NO logos in the image.
- Keep the center and lower-center area calm and empty (the rug area) — a boy character and
  a baby elephant will be placed there later, plus a big "Play" button.
- Keep the top edge simple (UI buttons will sit there).
- Background must support the scene, not distract a 2–5 year old child: moderate detail,
  soft depth of field on far objects.
- Format: 16:9, 1920x1080 (second version: 9:16, 1080x1920 with the window at the top and
  the rug in the lower third).
